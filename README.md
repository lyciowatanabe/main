# Hi, I'm Lycio Shinji Watanabe 👋

**Ph.D. in Analytical Chemistry** specializing in the intersection of **Complex Matrix Science, Chemometrics, and Applied Computing**. My professional and academic work focuses on developing innovative technological solutions, laboratory automation, analytical instrumentation, and **digital image analysis/processing using Artificial Intelligence (Deep Learning)**.

---

### 🎓 Academic Background

- 🎓 **Ph.D. in Analytical Chemistry** – UEPG / UNICENTRO / UEL Joint Program
- 🎓 **M.Sc. in Analytical Chemistry** – State University of Londrina (UEL)
- 🎓 **B.Sc. in Chemistry (Licentiate Degree)** – São Paulo State University (UNESP)

---

### 🔬 Research Areas & Expertise

- 📊 **Artificial Intelligence & Chemometrics:** Development and training of Convolutional Neural Networks (such as **VGG16** architectures) for automated digital image classification of food matrices (e.g., coffee beans) integrated with multivariate statistical analysis (PCA, HCA).
- 🧪 **Advanced Instrumental Analysis:** Extensive experience with chromatographic techniques and mass spectrometry (**LC-MS** and **GC-MS**) for the identification and quantification of metabolites, biomolecules, and detection of food fraud/adulteration.
- 🚀 **Industrial Innovation:** Direct involvement in the biological products sector, bioinputs, and intellectual property protection.
